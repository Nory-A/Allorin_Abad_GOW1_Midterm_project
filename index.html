PROJECT INTRODUCTION

Our Project

A.A Dictionary is a Web application powered by the Free Dictionary API, to give students, writers, and curious minds the meaning they need—quickly and clearly.

Our Main Goal

The main goal is to provide a simple and easy-to-use dictionary system where users can quickly find the meaning of a word. It aims to make searching for word definitions easier and more convenient.
